Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Litnest ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you visit our bookstore website, create an account, browse books, place an order, or use our services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with the practices described below, please do not use our website or services.

Our commitment: We collect only the information necessary to operate our bookstore system, process orders, provide customer support, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you register, place an order, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Personal details: Name, username, email address, and contact number.
  • Delivery information: Delivery address and other information needed to fulfill your order.
  • Account information: Username, password, and account preferences.
  • Order information: Books purchased, quantity, order date, order status, and total amount.
  • Payment information: Payment method and transaction details. Payments may be processed through secure third-party payment providers. We do not intentionally store complete payment credentials.
  • Communication data: Messages, inquiries, feedback, and other information submitted through our contact or support features.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit or use our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, books viewed, search activity, and time spent on the website.
  • Device information: Device type, browser type, and operating system.
  • Technical information: IP address and other basic information necessary for website security and functionality.
  • Cookies: Small files stored on your device that help improve your browsing experience and remember preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected through Litnest may be used for the following purposes:

  • To manage accounts: Create and maintain user accounts.
  • To process orders: Handle book purchases, payments, order confirmations, and deliveries.
  • To provide customer support: Respond to questions, concerns, complaints, and requests.
  • To improve our website: Analyze how users interact with the system and improve its features and functionality.
  • To personalize the experience: Remember user preferences and provide relevant book suggestions or search results.
  • To maintain security: Detect suspicious activity, unauthorized access, fraud, and other security concerns.
  • To send notifications: Provide order updates, delivery information, and other important service-related messages.
  • To comply with legal obligations: Maintain necessary records and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. However, certain information may be shared in limited circumstances when necessary to provide our services.

4.1 Delivery Providers

Information such as your name, contact number, delivery address, and order details may be shared with authorized delivery providers when necessary to complete your order.

4.2 Payment Providers

Payment-related information may be processed by trusted third-party payment providers. These providers handle payment information according to their own privacy and security policies.

4.3 Service Providers

We may work with third-party providers that assist with website hosting, maintenance, security, analytics, or other technical services. These providers may access only the information necessary to perform their services.

4.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose personal information when required by applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or other lawful government requests.

4.5 Business Transfers

If Litnest or its related business operations undergo a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of assets, user information may be transferred as part of the transaction, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, remember preferences, and understand how users interact with the system.

Types of cookies we may use include:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for basic functions such as account login and order processing.
  • Preference cookies: Remember settings and choices made by users.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand website traffic and improve the overall user experience.

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect some features of the website.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

These measures may include:

  • Password protection: User passwords are protected using appropriate security practices.
  • Secure connections: Sensitive information is transmitted through secure connections where applicable.
  • Access controls: Personal information is accessible only to authorized individuals who need it for legitimate purposes.
  • System monitoring: Security measures may be used to detect suspicious or unauthorized activity.
  • Data protection: Reasonable safeguards are implemented to protect information stored within the system.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no electronic storage system or Internet transmission is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain records, resolve concerns, improve the system, or comply with legal requirements.

Generally:

  • Account information: Retained while your account remains active.
  • Order information: Retained as necessary for order history, customer support, accounting, and record-keeping purposes.
  • Payment transaction records: Retained as necessary for transaction verification and financial records.
  • Communication records: May be retained to provide customer support and resolve disputes.
  • Usage information: May be retained in aggregated or anonymized form for analysis and system improvement.

When information is no longer needed, it may be deleted, anonymized, or securely disposed of.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable laws, users may have the following rights regarding their personal information:

  • Access: Request information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal or legitimate business requirements.
  • Restriction: Request limitations on certain types of data processing where applicable.
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of personal information where permitted by law.
  • Account updates: Modify or update certain account information through the website.

To exercise these rights or ask questions about how your information is handled, please contact us through the contact or support section of Litnest.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general users and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, please contact us. We will take reasonable steps to review and delete the information when required.

10. Third-Party Links

Litnest may contain links to third-party websites or services, such as payment providers, delivery services, or external resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of these external websites. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing personal information.

11. Data Transfers

Information may be processed or stored through third-party services located outside your local area, depending on the hosting, payment, analytics, or other technologies used by the system.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information is handled appropriately and protected in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in Litnest's services, system features, security practices, or applicable laws.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how their information is handled.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, requests, or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact us through the contact or support section of our website.

By using Litnest, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection and use of information as described above.
